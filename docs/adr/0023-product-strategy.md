# 0023: Product strategy: store-ready freemium, English-only, solo

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-02

## Context

The project needed a definition of success and explicit non-goals so effort went to the right place.

## Decision

North star is a mix of installs, DAU, and capture volume; first milestone is store-launch readiness. E-wallet and recurring-bill parsers are deferred (unreliable texts; card auto-debits already arrive via bank SMS; low ROI per parser). Freemium stays open: launch free/simple, no monetization plumbing. English-only UI. Single developer.

## Consequences

Deferred parsers are a conscious non-goal; monetization is decided later; the roadmap is scoped to one person’s capacity.
