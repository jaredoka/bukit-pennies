## 1. The service

Bukit Pennies parses bank notification text that you supply into a personal
spending record, dashboard and insights. It also lets you record budgets,
savings goals and subscriptions, and add transactions by hand.

It is an informational tool only: it is **not** a bank, a payment service, a
financial adviser, or an official record of your accounts. It never connects to
your bank and cannot move money. Your bank's statements remain the
authoritative record of your transactions, and nothing in the app is financial
advice.

Bukit Pennies is a free personal project, provided as-is. There are no paid
tiers, subscriptions or adverts.

## 2. Your account

You must be at least 13 years old to create an account.

You are responsible for keeping your password and capture tokens confidential.
Capture tokens act as bearer credentials for submitting text to your account —
treat them like passwords, and revoke any token you believe is exposed
(Settings → Capture → Capture devices).

Note that a capture token identifies the account that text is filed under, not
the person sending it. If you set up an automation on a shared device, spending
captured there is recorded against whichever account minted that token.

## 4. Accuracy

Parsing is best-effort. Messages the app is not confident about are routed to
your Review inbox rather than silently guessed, but errors can still occur —
verify important figures against your bank's records.

Two limits worth knowing:

- **Foreign currency is shown as the bank reported it.** The app does not
  convert between currencies, because the true amount your bank charges you,
  including its rate and fees, is not knowable from a notification message.
- **Subscriptions, budgets and goals you record are for your reference.** The
  amounts you enter there are never counted as spending; only real captured
  transactions are.
