# 0009: Remove the hornbill mascot

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-07-30

## Context

The mascot and its art generator were an AI-era leftover with no owner affection; the app needed a single clean brand mark.

## Decision

Remove the mascot from the app entirely; delete its art and generator scripts from art/. The owner draws replacement pixel art by hand; the penny coin is the only brand mark until then. Do not reintroduce a mascot or regenerate the bird.

## Consequences

The penny coin becomes the brand; any future mascot is hand-drawn by the owner, never AI-generated.
